--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. ONE-PARAGRAPH STATE OF THE PROJECT (read this first)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We argue a glass-substrate photonic FlattenedButterfly ("glass-FB") is the
right switchless interconnect for large-EP MoE serving. CRITICAL methodology
fact: our backend (ASTRA-Sim) is CONGESTION-UNAWARE, so MEASURED end-to-end
decode/prefill at TP=1 shows glass ~= NVL72 PARITY — the simulator cannot turn
the inter-domain bandwidth gap into a visible TPOT cliff (the MoE all-to-all is
~5% of a compute-bound step, and ASTRA divides collective volume by domain count
so the slow-link penalty washes out). This is the SAME op/collective-composition
methodology NVIDIA's aiconfigurator uses, so it is defensible, not a weakness.
The fabric/topology advantage is therefore argued on FOUR axes that do NOT
require contention modeling: (1) ANALYTICAL collective latency / bisection
(optical 512 vs IB 50 -> ~10x at the cliff EP), (2) ENERGY-per-token, (3)
physical FEASIBILITY (micro-bump / WG budget, glass-only long links), and (4)
TOPOLOGY structure (diameter / avg-hop). The current headline being built is the
TOPOLOGY one (see Sec 5C) under the "FB is the glass-optimal topology" framing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THESIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Claim: A glass-photonic FlattenedButterfly fabric lets large-MoE inference scale
expert parallelism (EP) PAST the NVLink domain boundary without the inter-domain
InfiniBand (IB) penalty that throttles NVLink-class systems, AND it is the
cost/feasibility-optimal switchless topology for doing so.

Why it matters: production MoE (DeepSeek-V3 256 experts, Qwen3-235B 128 experts,
Kimi-K2) is served at very large EP, which spans many NVLink domains. Beyond the
domain, EP all-to-all falls onto IB (~50 GB/s) — far slower than in-domain
NVLink. Glass-FB connects panels over optical links (~512 GB/s) + an optical
circuit switch, keeping the MoE all-to-all cheap as EP scales.

Two related works anchor methodology (both orthogonal to us):
  - ViBE (Go, Mahajan et al.): co-optimizes expert PLACEMENT under skew +
    hardware variability, interconnect FIXED. We FIX placement, vary the
    INTERCONNECT. We sit exactly where their placement lever saturates (>~64 GPU).
  - NVIDIA aiconfigurator (arXiv 2601.06288): op-level config DSE, a
    goodput-vs-interactivity Pareto under an SLA. SAME op/collective decomposition
    as us; we MIRROR its Pareto axes. Our novelty = we OPEN the fabric/topology
    design axis that aiconfigurator (and ViBE) take as FIXED.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYSTEM DESIGN (glass-FB) — PHYSICAL DESIGN CONSTANTS (confirmed)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Waveguide: 1 WG = 32 lambda x 32 Gb/s = 1.024 Tb/s = 128 GB/s UNIDIRECTIONAL.
  WG is unidirectional -> duplex link = N_WG TX + N_WG RX. TOTAL bundle = 2*N_WG
  (always even; TX/RX symmetric).
- Convention split (CRITICAL): sweep CLI `--fixed-wg` = TOTAL bundle (even);
  config_builder `wg_count` = per-direction = TOTAL/2; intra_opt_bw = wg_count*128.
  e.g. --fixed-wg 8 -> per-dir 4 -> 512 GB/s; --fixed-wg 10 -> 640.
- Panel = 2D grid of GPUs wired as FlattenedButterfly (1-2 hop intra-panel).
  4x4 = 16 GPU; 4x8 = 32; 8x8 = 64. Inter-panel = OPTICAL CIRCUIT SWITCH
  (FullyConnected, single switched hop, 512 GB/s) — NOT a ring.
- Micro-bump budget: tile 1600 mm^2 x 1.083 bumps/mm^2 ~ 1733 bumps -> ~54-60
  WG/GPU (both directions). Per-grid N_WG cap = budget/degree/2:
      4x4 (degree 6): cap 4/dir (floor) .. 5 (ceil) -> bundle x8..x10
      6x6/6x6-4c (deg 10): cap 2..3/dir -> x4..x6
      8x8 (deg 14): cap 1..2/dir -> x4
  Higher-degree grids split the budget thinner (this matters a LOT — see 5C).
- Power: optical CPO ~1.15 pJ/bit; interconnect_power = total_WG*1.024e12*1.15e-12.
  x6 (60 WG/GPU) ~71 W/GPU. PASSIVE fabric (no switch power). vs NVL72 active
  NVSwitch 540 W/rack (15 W * 36). per-GPU aggregate BW glass 7.68 vs NVL72 1.8
  TB/s = 4.3x.
- Latency: glass intra-panel optical hop ~100 ns (TeraPHY CPO, switchless);
  inter-panel fiber ~500 ns; NVL72 NVSwitch hop ~500 ns. (inter-panel 500 vs
  5000 ns is <1% on TPOT — BW-bound — so robust.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. BASELINE (NVLink) — H100-CONSISTENT primary (compute is H100-profiled)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because per-layer latencies come from an H100 profiler, the interconnect baseline
must also be H100-consistent (not GB200) for the primary result:
  - PRIMARY (H100): NVLink4 = 450 GB/s unidir (900 bidir/2), NVLink domain = 8
    GPU (HGX island), IB 50 GB/s @ EP>8, 500 ns hop.
    flags: --nvl72-bw 450 --nvl72-rack 8 ; energy --baseline h100.
    -> glass wins both in-domain BW (optical 512 > 450) and domain size (panel
    16 > island 8); the cliff is pulled to EP>8 so small-EP fabric differences show.
  - FORWARD-LOOKING SENSITIVITY (GB200 NVL72): NVLink5 = 900 GB/s unidir, domain
    64, IB 50 @ EP>64. flags: --nvl72-bw 900 --nvl72-rack 64 ; --baseline gb200.
    Compute still H100 (approx) -> disclose in limitations.
NOTE: the topo_scale/topo_prefill runs print a GB200 baseline line, but NVL72 is
NOT a topology in the topo_compare plots — topology comparison is glass-only
(switchless regime; NVLink/NVSwitch is a logical fully-connected switch, has no
"topology"). NVL72 appears only in the FABRIC (FB-vs-NVL72) collective/energy figs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SIMULATOR & METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tool: LLMServingSim 2.0 = Python serving frontend (vLLM-style continuous batching,
chunked prefill, radix prefix cache) + ASTRA-Sim analytical network backend
(Chakra .et). Per-layer latencies from a vLLM layerwise profiler (real H100/H200
kernel timings), NOT analytic FLOPs. EP is a runtime/topology knob (MoE profiled
once at tp=1; per-rank latency = f(local_tokens, activated_experts)).

Models: DeepSeek-V3-0324 (671B, 256 experts, 8 routed, MLA), Qwen3-235B-A22B
(235B, 128 experts, GQA), Kimi-K2 (MLA), Qwen3-30B-A3B (fast proxy, up to EP256).

MoE collective model (MOE_ALLTOALL=1): large-EP MoE = true DeepEP all-to-all.
Multi-domain emits BOTH legs: within-domain (AllGather/ReduceScatter over inner
fast dim) + cross-domain (ALLTOALL over outer slow dim @ IB or inter-panel optical).

Phase-aware parse (sweep_panel_dse._phase_split, REGRESSION-TESTED): decode steps
(small) and prefill chunks (large) are bimodal; split = small-cluster MEDIAN +
core-band [0.5,1.5]x median for exposed (robust to spread / dp-sync stalls).
Outputs decode_step_ms / decode_exposed_frac AND prefill_step_ms /
prefill_exposed_frac. (Flipped phase 3x before this fix; locked by
scripts/test_phase_split.py.)

CONTROLLED-burst caveat: all requests arrive t=0 -> TTFT is a thundering-herd
QUEUE artifact (e.g. 8 requests/instance pile up -> TTFT ~ 8x one prefill chunk).
TTFT absolute is NOT online TTFT. Use per-CHUNK prefill_step_ms (queue-immune) and
decode tpot_gt_ms; realistic TTFT needs Poisson (slo_eval). Sanity: DeepSeek
2048-tok prefill chunk ~290 ms at TP=1 (FLOP-checked, 2*37e9*2048/550TFLOPS~270ms);
decode step ~35 ms = weight-load-bound (EP16 113 GB / 3350 GB/s = 33.7 ms). Both
are TP=1-INFLATED (TP=8 ~8x lower) — see Sec 7 TP scope.

Energy: SEPARATE post-hoc model scripts/energy_model.py (NOT a built-in --power
flag). Sourced glass constants (TX 1.66 + RX 0.96 = 2.62 pJ/bit dynamic, laser
N_lambda*1mW/WPE, thermal tuning). Baselines {h100: domain8/12.5 W, gb200:
domain64/8.44 W per-GPU NVSwitch}.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. KEY RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5A. MEASURED END-TO-END (decode & prefill, TP=1) = PARITY, BY DESIGN OF THE SIM.
    glass ~= NVL72 within ~1-2% on decode TPOT and on prefill chunk time at TP=1.
    This is EXPECTED and CORRECT: congestion-unaware ASTRA cannot convert the
    inter-domain bandwidth gap into a measured cliff. CONCLUSION: do NOT headline
    a measured TPOT cliff. (The old Sec-5a "reach cliff" table in prior versions
    of this file was a RING-artifact + congestion-unaware over-read; DISCARDED.)

5B. FABRIC (glass-FB vs NVL72) — ANALYTICAL collective latency. The IB cliff
    lives in the collective-latency metric (msg / link-bw), not in TPOT:
      all_to_all_us(NVL72) / all_to_all_us(FB) ~ 10x at the EP where NVL72 crosses
      its NVLink domain onto IB (optical 512 vs IB 50). Plot: plot_collective.py
      --metric a2a on a reach CSV (Fig B). This + energy + feasibility carry the
      FB-vs-NVL72 story since measured TPOT is parity.

5C. TOPOLOGY (glass-only: FB vs Dragonfly/Torus/Mesh/Ring) — THE CURRENT HEADLINE.
    Two fairness regimes (this is the crux):

    (i) iso-BUDGET (same total WG/GPU = 60, split by degree). High-degree FB gets
        THIN per-link BW; low-degree mesh/ring get FAT links.
        MEASURED (DeepSeek, prefill chunk 2048, single-panel-per-EP):
          prefill_exposed: FB 14.0% > mesh/torus 11.6%, ring 37.1%  (EP16)
          TTFT(diameter proxy) FB vs mesh: +3% (EP16) -> +8.5% (EP32) -> +14% (EP64)
        => Under iso-budget in a BANDWIDTH-BOUND congestion-unaware sim, FB
        actually FALLS BEHIND mesh as EP grows (its degree (rows-1)+(cols-1) climbs
        6->10->14, thinning links), while only RING clearly loses (diameter).
        This is the conservative/cost-fair view and it does NOT favor FB.

    (ii) iso-PER-LINK (--equal-link-bw 512). Every topology same per-link BW; this
        is what glass ENABLES (abundant cheap long links -> FB can afford its high
        radix). MEASURED (DeepSeek, decode regime so endpoints only):
          decode_exposed: FB 2.5% ~= mesh 2.6% (EP16), 4.5 ~= 4.8 (EP32);
          RING 19.9% (EP16) -> 40.4% (EP32) — diameter laid BARE (verified: ring
          diameter 16 hops * msg/512 * 61 layers * 2 ~ 27 ms exposed @ EP32).
        => Stripping the fat-link crutch crushes low-radix RING and removes FB's
        penalty. decode still can't separate FB-vs-mesh (compute-bound ~2.5%);
        the FB<mesh separation needs PREFILL+iso-per-link (PENDING run:
        EQBW=512 run_topo_prefill_hpc.sh).

    (iii) ANALYTICAL avg-hop (topo_project.py): FB diameter ->2, Dragonfly ->3
        (scale-FLAT); Torus/Mesh ~sqrt(N), Ring ~N/4 (DIVERGE). FB lowest
        throughout; at N=256 FB 1x / Dragonfly 1.5x / Torus 3.9x / Mesh 5.2x /
        Ring 31.5x. Congestion-unaware ASTRA's per-pair send == avg-hop model, so
        this projection is faithful and sidesteps the bandwidth-bound measured limit.

    *** FRAMING (confirmed direction) ***
    "Under an artificial equal-budget cap, glass's wiring abundance is hidden and
     mesh/ring stay competitive. Under equal per-link bandwidth — what glass
     actually enables (abundant cheap long links) — high-radix FB dominates while
     low-radix ring cannot exploit the abundance. FB is the glass-optimal topology."
    Supporting sub-claims: FB is diameter-optimal (2, below even Dragonfly's 3) AND
    uniquely in-package glass-realizable (long uniform row/col links need glass 3D
    distance-layered routing; Dragonfly globals are inter-package cables). RING is
    the cautionary opposite — it cannot exploit glass's abundance. Contribution =
    feasibility + Pareto positioning, NOT a naive "FB beats mesh at any cost".

5D. ENERGY (post-hoc energy_model.py). Glass interconnect energy is a small
    fraction of NVSwitch on the LINK component; combined with faster completion the
    TOTAL energy-per-token favors glass at scale. (Re-run pending under the
    H100-consistent baseline; --baseline h100. Needs a defensible NVSwitch W cite.)

5E. SLO / Pareto (aiconfigurator axes: x = tok/s/user = 1000/TPOT, y = tok/s/GPU).
    DEFERRED to TP=8 — at TP=1 the dense/MLA compute is unsharded so TPOT ~35 ms ->
    interactivity ~28 tok/s/user, below AFD's ~100+. The RELATIVE ordering is
    TP-robust but ABSOLUTE interactivity needs TP=8 (see Sec 7).

6. KEY MODELING DECISIONS & REVISIONS (threats-to-validity / methodology section)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) MLA KV fix (CRITICAL, this session). DeepSeek/Kimi KV was modeled as MHA
    (2*kv_dim = 32768/tok/layer) — a 57x OVER-count. MLA stores a compressed latent
    (kv_lora_rank 512 + qk_rope_head_dim 64 = 576/tok/layer, replicated, no
    //num_npus). Fixed in memory_model get_kv / full_cluster_kv_bytes_per_token and
    calculate_sizes('qkv_proj') (MLA q_a/kv_a/kv_b branch, 5x weight over-count
    also fixed). Result: DeepSeek now FITS at EP>=16 (EP8 ~199 GB, EP16 113, EP32
    67, EP128 38). Qwen (GQA) path unchanged. MLA_KV_OFF env toggle kept for A/B.
    Must keep dense/GQA/MLA ALL working (not MLA-only).
(b) Congestion-unaware acceptance (CRITICAL reframe). ASTRA divides collective
    volume by domain count and has no link contention -> measured end-to-end can't
    show the IB/topology bandwidth advantage. This is STANDARD (aiconfigurator does
    the same). Pivot: ANALYTICAL collective-latency / avg-hop headline + MEASURED
    as supporting (resolves endpoints: ring loses, FB best at iso-per-link).
(c) H100-consistent baseline (this session). Switched primary NVLink baseline from
    GB200 NVL72 (900/domain64) to H100 NVLink4 (450/domain8) because compute is
    H100-profiled; GB200 kept as forward-looking sensitivity. --nvl72-bw/--nvl72-
    rack/--baseline flags.
(d) Metric pivot to collective-latency / prefill exposed (this session). Decode
    TPOT hides the fabric (comm ~5% of a compute-bound step). 1st-class metric =
    collective latency / exposed comm, measured in PREFILL (large msgs expose
    diameter). plot_collective.py adds prefill_exposed / prefill_tpot metrics.
(e) Phase-split robustness (this session). The decode/prefill split flipped phase
    across fabrics 3x (median-guard -> min-anchor -> mode-on-spread). Final: small-
    cluster MEDIAN + core-band [0.5,1.5]x. Locked by test_phase_split.py.
(f) TP>1 deadlock scope. TP>1 deadlocks ONLY the dp_group MULTI-INSTANCE path
    (cross-instance .et stream misalign). Single-instance TP+EP (ViBE 8-GPU
    num_npus=8/tp8/ep8) WORKS — needs only tp8 profiling. Topology comparison is
    inherently multi-instance (panel of instances) -> TP=1 only.
(g) Older fixes retained: glass inter-panel Ring->FullyConnected (optical switch =
    1 hop, bandwidth-bound, not hop-count); within+cross a2a legs; add_done DP+EP
    completion flag (bench-vs-vLLM EP=2 TPOT +1.1%); EP128 needs HPC (OOM, .et graph
    super-linear in NPU count); float comm_size wrapped int() for the re-parser.

7. TP SCOPE (why most absolute numbers are TP=1, what TP=8 unlocks)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Topology comparison = a PANEL of single-GPU instances => inherently multi-
  instance => TP=1 (TP>1 multi-instance deadlocks, 6f). The RELATIVE topology
  ordering (ring loses; FB-vs-mesh) is TP-ROBUST, so the topology headline is valid
  at TP=1.
- Absolute step times are TP=1-INFLATED: decode ~35 ms (weight-load), prefill chunk
  ~290 ms (compute) -> interactivity ~28 tok/s/user. TP=8 shards dense/MLA -> ~8x
  lower -> production-realistic interactivity / SLO.
- DEFERRED to when TP=8 profiling lands: ViBE single-instance tp8/ep8 serving runs,
  SLO/Pareto absolute interactivity (run_slo_hpc.sh, run_exposed_batch_hpc.sh).
  configs/cluster/vibe_tp8_ep8_{deepseek,qwen}.json are ready. Multi-panel TP>1
  would additionally need the deadlock fix (option B: single-instance multi-dim).

9. CAVEATS / LIMITATIONS (disclose)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Congestion-unaware backend: measured end-to-end is bandwidth/avg-hop, no link
  contention -> measured glass~=NVL72 parity; fabric/topology advantage argued
  analytically + structurally. (Same as aiconfigurator.)
- TP=1 for topology/absolute numbers (TP>1 multi-instance deadlocks). Absolute
  TPOT/interactivity inflated ~8x vs TP=8; the glass:baseline RATIO / topology
  ORDERING is the result. SLO absolutes deferred to TP=8.
- Controlled-burst TTFT = queue artifact; use prefill_step_ms + Poisson slo_eval.
- Engine validated vs real vLLM only at EP=2 (+1.1% TPOT); EP>2 are projections.
- iso-budget vs iso-per-link are TWO fairness lenses; we report BOTH and argue the
  glass premise (cheap abundant links) justifies iso-per-link as the operative one.
- Energy/power constants first-cut/cited (PanelScale, datasheets) — TUNE; NVSwitch
  W needs a firm cite.
